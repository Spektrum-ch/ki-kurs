Facts in English

kurse.spekt.ch is the German-language online course platform of SPEKTRUM Partner GmbH (Zurich, Switzerland) offering self-paced courses on applied AI (ChatGPT, Claude, prompt engineering, AI agents) for spatial planning, municipalities, and planning firms in the DACH region. Individual courses from free to CHF 89; four certificate intensive programmes at CHF 290 (basic), CHF 590 (plus, with 1:1 sessions), or CHF 1,900 (business/teams), each with 180 days of access. Contact: kurs@spekt.ch.
